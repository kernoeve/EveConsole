# Code of Conduct

EVE Console is a small project with a small community. This document exists so that if something
goes wrong, nobody has to guess what the standard was or who to tell.

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith, and give them
the benefit of the doubt you would want.

## What is expected

- Treat other people with respect, including when you think they are wrong
- Accept that a maintainer's time is finite, and that "no" is a legitimate answer to a request
- Give feedback about the work rather than about the person doing it
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, and fix it

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private
- Insults, personal attacks, or comments directed at someone's identity — including their race,
  gender, sexuality, disability, nationality, religion, age or appearance
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion, issues or pull requests

This is a tool for a video game with a robust culture. In-game rivalry, scamming stories and the
usual EVE gallows humour are not what this is about — but they stop at the point where a real
person is the target.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project is: this repository, its issues and pull requests, the
[Discord server](https://discord.gg/H6NaAjJMar), and anywhere someone is representing EVE Console
in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness something that breaks this, tell me directly: **DM `Kerno` on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/H6NaAjJMar)** — I am the server owner, so a direct message reaches me
without going through anyone else.

I will read every report and keep it confidential. If a report concerns me, or you would rather not
raise it with me, tell one of the Discord moderators instead.

Reports are taken seriously whether or not the person reporting has contributed code.

## What happens after a report

I will respond as soon as I reasonably can — this is a side project, so that is measured in days
rather than hours.

Depending on what happened, the outcome may be a private word, a public correction, having content
removed, a temporary suspension from the Discord or the repository, or a permanent ban. I will
explain the decision to the person who reported it.

## A note on enforcement

There is one maintainer, and no committee to appeal to. I will try to be fair and to explain my
reasoning, but the honest position is that this comes down to one person's judgement. If you think
I have got it wrong, say so — I would rather be told.
