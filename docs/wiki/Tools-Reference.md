# Tools Reference

A quick tour of what each tool does. Most depend on having a
[market configured](Configuring-Markets) and an [industry park](Industry-Parks) set up.

## Industry / Trade

- **Market Levels** — monitor a defined market for sell-order inventory across a
  list of items.
- **Inventory Levels** — monitor your own holdings (on hand, in build, on buy
  orders, etc.) against a defined list, similar to jEveAssets stockpiles.
- **Trade Opportunities** — compare markets to surface profitable moves between
  them.
- **Net Worth** — a running chart of your total value over time.
- **Production Calculator** — plan production jobs: build costs, materials
  needed, and multi-level breakdowns. Can restrict the picker to producible items
  and optionally include the final blueprint copy (BPC) cost.

## Corporation

- **Activity Monitor** — income from ratting, industry, mining, kills, and corp
  project activity.
- **Corp Projects** — active and historical corp project details.
- **Standing Projects** — persistent goals you always want maintained (e.g. keep
  ADM below a threshold in a region).
- **Top 10 Lists** — activity leaderboards from corp ESI data.

## Browsers & other

- **Character Viewer** — an in-app character sheet.
- **Item Browser** — every item in the game with descriptions/attributes, plus
  current market orders and price history for your defined markets.
- **Asset Viewer** — search across all personal and corp assets.
- **Killmail Viewer** — corp and personal killmails.
- **Alerts** — the main overview surfaces things the app thinks you should look
  at (configurable).
- **Eve Mail** — read and write EVE mail.
