# Industry Parks

An **industry park** describes the structures you actually build in, so that
build-cost and production calculations reflect your real bonuses (material
efficiency, rig bonuses, structure type, etc.) instead of generic values.

## Key concepts

- **Per-category structures** — assign different structures to different item
  categories (e.g. one setup for capital components, another for ammo).
- **Per-item exceptions** — override the structure for specific items that don't
  fit their category's default.
- Parks feed the system-wide **build cost** that Eve Cortex calculates and stores
  for every craftable item, which in turn feeds the [Production Calculator](Tools-Reference)
  and the build-cost floor in [market pricing](Configuring-Markets).
