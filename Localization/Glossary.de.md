# Deutsch glossary

The terms EVE Console's German text uses, so the same word reads the same on every screen and
matches the game where the game has a word for it. Corrections from German-speaking players are
welcome — this list is where they belong, so every screen picks them up.

## Style

- Formal **„Sie“**, as the German client. Buttons and menu items are infinitives (`Speichern`,
  `Hinzufügen`); tooltips and messages are complete sentences.
- Nouns capitalised as always. A label ending in `:` keeps its `:`. Quotation marks „…“ (inside
  them ‚…‘).
- A dash between parts of a sentence is the German Gedankenstrich with spaces: ` – ` where the
  English has ` — `.
- Compounds with a name, an abbreviation or an English word take a hyphen: `ESI-Token`,
  `EVE-Mail`, `SDE-Daten`, `LP-Shop`, `Indy-Park`, `KI-Assistent`.
- Numbers follow the German region (`1.234,5`), in fixed text too (`1,0 – 0,5`). A `%` written
  after a number takes a space (`{0} %`). In a narrow header an average is `Ø` and an estimate
  `ca.`.
- Durations in the client's short units: `Tg.`, `Std.`, `Min.`, `Sek.` (`2 Tg. 5 Std. 30 Min.`,
  `vor 5 Min.`). The short number suffixes K / M / B stay, as the client shows them.
- Dates: day first with a full stop — `d. MMM`, `d. MMM yyyy`, `d. MMM yyyy, HH:mm`,
  `dddd, d. MMM yyyy  HH:mm`, `MMMM yyyy`. Inside a placeholder too: `{0:d MMM HH:mm}` becomes
  `{0:d. MMM HH:mm}`, `{0:ddd d MMM HH:mm}` becomes `{0:ddd, d. MMM HH:mm}`; `yyyy-MM-dd` stays.
- **Placeholders stay exactly as written**: `{0}`, `{1:N0}`, `{alarm}`. They may move in the
  sentence; none may be dropped or added.
- A count in a fixed text whose German plural changes the word (Auftrag / Aufträge, Durchlauf /
  Durchläufe) goes after a colon (`Aufträge: {0:N0}`) or into an abbreviation (`Durchl.`); a
  counted phrase with One / Other entries takes the proper forms.
- **Unchanged**: EVE Console, EVE, ESI, SDE, SSO, CCP, ISK, m³, ME, TE, SP, PI, zKillboard,
  Fuzzwork, Slack, Discord, GitHub, Tranquility, keyboard keys (`Ctrl+C`, `Enter`), file formats,
  URLs and anything that is typed exactly (`yyyy-MM-dd`, ESI scope names).
- Also left in English, because they are typed, matched or shown in English: the store's mail
  commands (PRICES, ORDER, STATUS, CANCEL, HELP, INFO), the ship classes the alarms match
  (Freighter, Jump Freighter, Hauler), Slack emoji codes, and the labels of English-only sites
  (EVE developer site, Cloudflare dashboard, api.slack.com), quoted as they appear:
  „Create New App“. Skill names, Standup module names, Keepstar / Fortizar and materials such as
  Magmatic Gas stay English, as the German client shows them.
- English written in capitals is a small heading; in German it is written in normal case.
- Column headers and buttons short — German runs long; prefer the shorter of two good words, and
  an abbreviation with a full stop (`Anz.`, `Std.`) where a header is narrow: `ges.` for gesamt
  (`Einnahmen ges.`), or the word cut after a syllable (`Durchl.`, `Übersch.`, `Restbelohn.`).
  A header may drop what its column makes obvious (`Verkauft 30 Tg.` beside `Umsatz 30 Tg.`).
  Measure it: the app cuts off what does not fit.
- A tool named in a sentence or tooltip goes in quotes when its name is a plural noun:
  `In „NPC-Entitäten“ öffnen`, `In „Spielerentitäten“ öffnen`; the browsers read
  `Im Gegenstandsbrowser öffnen`, `Im Strukturbrowser öffnen`.

## For a native speaker to check

Open questions from the drafting, by area, the most important first. Each line: the entry, the
English, the draft, the doubt. **meaning** = the sense was inferred; **client** = the draft
differs from the German client's word, or should follow it; **fit** = shortened for its space;
**style** = wording.

### Wallet journal types (RefTypeText)

- **meaning** `RefTypeText.MarketProviderTax` "Market Provider Tax" → „Marktbetreibersteuer“ — the client has no name for it; the sense is guessed.
- **meaning** `RefTypeText.EssEscrowTransfer` "ESS Transfer" → „ESS-Treuhandzahlung“ — no client name; ESI calls it ess_escrow_transfer, read as a payout from the ESS reserve.
- **meaning** `RefTypeText.StructureGateJump` "Structure Gate Jump" → „Strukturtor-Sprung“ — no client name; presumably the fee for an Ansiblex jump.
- **meaning** `RefTypeText.MilestoneRewardPayment` / `AchievementMilestoneReward` / `AchievementCategoryMilestoneReward` → „Auszahlung der Meilensteinbelohnung“ / „Meilensteinbelohnung für Erfolg“ / „… für Erfolgskategorie“ — no client names.
- **meaning** `RefTypeText.DailyChallengeReward` / `ResourceWarsReward` / `GmPlexFeeRefund` / `MiningTax` / `ProjectPayouts` → „Belohnung für tägliche Herausforderung“ / „Ressourcenkrieg-Belohnung“ / „GM PLEX-Gebührenrückerstattung“ / „Bergbausteuer“ / „Projektauszahlungen“ — no client names; GM-PLEX-… would follow the hyphen rule.
