# Deutsch glossary

The terms EVE Console's German text uses, so the same word reads the same on every screen and
matches the game where the game has a word for it. Corrections from German-speaking players are
welcome — this list is where they belong, so every screen picks them up.

## Style

- Formal **„Sie“**, as the German client. Buttons and menu items are infinitives (`Speichern`,
  `Hinzufügen`); tooltips and messages are complete sentences.
- Nouns capitalised as always. A label ending in `:` keeps its `:`. Quotation marks „…“ (inside
  them ‚…‘).
- A dash between parts of a sentence is the German Gedankenstrich with spaces: ` – ` where the
  English has ` — `.
- Compounds with a name, an abbreviation or an English word take a hyphen: `ESI-Token`,
  `EVE-Mail`, `SDE-Daten`, `LP-Shop`, `Indy-Park`, `KI-Assistent`.
- Numbers follow the German region (`1.234,5`). A `%` written after a number takes a space
  (`{0} %`).
- Durations in the client's short units: `Tg.`, `Std.`, `Min.`, `Sek.` (`2 Tg. 5 Std. 30 Min.`,
  `vor 5 Min.`). The short number suffixes K / M / B stay, as the client shows them.
- Dates: day first with a full stop — `d. MMM`, `d. MMM yyyy`, `d. MMM yyyy, HH:mm`,
  `dddd, d. MMM yyyy  HH:mm`, `MMMM yyyy`.
- **Placeholders stay exactly as written**: `{0}`, `{1:N0}`, `{alarm}`. They may move in the
  sentence; none may be dropped or added.
- **Unchanged**: EVE Console, EVE, ESI, SDE, SSO, CCP, ISK, m³, ME, TE, SP, PI, zKillboard,
  Fuzzwork, Slack, Discord, GitHub, Tranquility, keyboard keys (`Ctrl+C`, `Enter`), file formats,
  URLs and anything that is typed exactly (`yyyy-MM-dd`, ESI scope names).
- English written in capitals is a small heading; in German it is written in normal case.
- Column headers and buttons short — German runs long; prefer the shorter of two good words, and
  an abbreviation with a full stop (`Anz.`, `Std.`) where a header is narrow.
