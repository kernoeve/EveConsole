# 简体中文 glossary

The terms EVE Console's Chinese text uses, so the same word reads the same on every screen and
matches the game where the game has a word for it. Corrections from Chinese-speaking players are
welcome — this list is where they belong, so every screen picks them up.

## Style

- Simplified Chinese, short and plain, as in a desktop app. No full stop after a label or a button.
- Chinese punctuation inside Chinese text: `，。：；！？（）“”`. A label ending in `:` ends in `：`.
- A space between Chinese and Latin letters or numbers: `ESI 令牌`, `30 天`.
- **Placeholders stay exactly as written**: `{0}`, `{1:N0}`, `{alarm}`. They may move in the sentence;
  none may be dropped or added.
- **Unchanged**: EVE Console, EVE, ESI, SDE, SSO, CCP, ISK, m³, ME, TE, zKillboard, Fuzzwork, Slack,
  Discord, GitHub, Tranquility (first mention in a sentence may add （宁静）), keyboard keys, file
  formats, URLs and anything that is typed exactly (`yyyy-MM-dd`).
- English written in capitals is a small heading; in Chinese it is simply the words.
